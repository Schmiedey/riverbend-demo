# What the demo does — for the sales email

Plain-language bullets for a coach or tournament director. Paste into the email body under the demo link.

- **Teams register and pay in one go, from a phone.** About two minutes, no paper forms, no chasing checks. It catches typos (like a bad email) before they cost you a day of back-and-forth.
- **Coaches get a confirmation email and receipt right away,** plus a one-tap "add to calendar" for the weekend.
- **Brackets and standings are a web page, not a PDF.** Readable on a phone in the dugout with no pinch-zooming, and they update as scores come in.
- **Pool play seeds itself.** Enter scores and the standings and tiebreakers (wins, runs allowed, run differential) work out who plays who on Sunday.
- **Coaches can filter the schedule to just their team** and see the field map, so fewer "which field are we on?" calls.
- **You get a dashboard of every team:** who's paid, who hasn't, a one-click payment reminder, and an export to Excel or Google Sheets.
- **Payments go through Stripe** straight to your account. No middleman holding your money.
- **It's fast and cheap to run:** loads in about a second on a phone, with no monthly software fee beyond Stripe's standard card processing.
