Riverbend Fall Classic

12U & 14U baseball
November 7–8 · Riverbend Park, Millbrook

Register a team — $495

Sixteen teams, four lit fields inside a bend of the river, one November weekend. Everybody plays Sunday.

Three pool games each on Saturday seed all eight teams per age group into Sunday's bracket, and the bracket updates from the field the minute a game ends. Coaches register, pay and find their next game from a phone.

Boys playing baseball, 1923. Library of Congress. The game hasn't changed much; the paperwork can.

How the weekend runs

By October 30Register online: team details, the $495 entry by card, and a receipt in the coach's inbox before they've closed the tab. Sixteen spots, fourteen already paid.

Saturday, November 7Three pool games for every team, 90 minutes each, first pitch at 8:00. Standings update after every final, tiebreakers worked out for you.

Sunday, November 8All eight teams in each age group play on, seeded into a single-elimination bracket. Semifinals at 12:30, championships at 3:00 under the lights.

What happens if it rains?

Games pause for lightning within eight miles and restart 30 minutes after the last strike. Delays post on the bracket page and go by text to every head coach.

How old can players be?

12U: born in 2014 or later. 14U: born in 2012 or later. Bring birth-year proof to check-in.

Can we change our roster?

Yes, free, until you check in on Saturday. Rosters run 9 to 15 players.

Do you refund entry fees?

In full until October 30. If weather cancels the event, you get a credit toward next fall.

Where do we warm up?

Batting cages behind Field 3 and the grass along the river. Book a 20-minute cage slot at check-in.
