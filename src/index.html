How the weekend works

  • Saturday: four pools of four. Every team plays three 90-minute pool games.
  • Sunday: all eight teams in each age group are seeded into a single-elimination bracket.
  • Seeding: wins, then fewest runs allowed, then run differential. Standings update as scores come in.
  • Schedules live here, not in a PDF. Check the bracket and field times from your phone in the dugout.
